Technology Futures Dashboard

Finally, an interactive guide to the future of AI that pushes back on the doomer stories like AI2027 and enables more realistic scenario planning based both on the realities of AI's economic benefit and your inputs and judgement on the rate of technological control, AI regulation and overall technological change. This chose your own adventure dashboard can help you better conceptualize the incredible economic growth and benefits to humanity coming through technology acceleration.

How to use this dashboard
Run future scenarios on the impact of technology acceleration on things that matter. Start with one of four major futures (Walled Gardens, Open Frontiers, State Machine, Wild West). Then use sliders to adjust your opinions on technology acceleration to see impact on projected future metrics. View scenario description summary in concluding summary block.
